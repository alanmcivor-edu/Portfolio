Uploads are selections of figures and technical documentation from three capstone design projects. All three projects were completed for capstone design courses within the Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering department at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), between January 2022 and June 2021.
Two of the three courses, aircraft and spacecraft design, were the final courses in a four-course spacecraft or aircraft engineering track. The third course, rocket design, was the final course in the major field.
